# The Scoop

## Opening Scene

Your adventuring group has been summoned to the mayor's office. Upon arrival, you notice there are guards surrounding the door. Once your identities have been confirmed, they step aside and let you enter the office. The man behind the desk seems quite nervous about something.

"Ah! You are the adventurers I summoned. Better late than never. You see, I, Buster Bravo, need your help. Someone is out to get me. I know they're planning to capture me and take me prisoner. Of course, this is politically motivated. My re-election campaign is in full swing, and the opposition does not want me to be re-elected. My term so far has been spectacular, with no complaints, and improvements in quality of life for all. Thus, the other side wants me out of the picture, and I can't be sworn in for another term if I'm missing. You've got to put a stop to it! It's madness!"

The players should ask for more information as to who is plotting this.

"Ah yes, of course, you are not as aware of these things as I am, seeing as you're commoners and not elite politicians such as myself. For that I will forgive you."

"No matter how uninititated you are in the world of politics, having accepted this invitation, you must know that I lead the ruling Bravo party. I have been in power for almost five years, the length of a term in office. The main opposing party is the Sierra party, which seeks to ruin all that we have gained these last few years! Mind you, there are some other smaller parties, but none of them would seek to do such a heinous crime against me."

The players might ask for information regarding how the mayor knows something is afoot.

"I received a letter in the mail with no return address. It asked me to come ALONE to the town center at 9pm tonight. There is absolutely no reason mentioned. And why specify alone? It must be a kidnapping attempt! Why would anyone fall for that? Regardless of my intentions to not follow through, these perpetrators must be punished!"

The players should ask where to find the Sierra party.

"They have a campaign headquarters on Tarry Street just to the east of here. You should seek justice for anyone involved in this scheme! And do not listen to anything they say! They're just lying to get away with this attempt on my life!"

"Off you go! No need to dally here. Go do the job, and I will see that you are rewarded."

The guards enter the office and escort you out.

(Players should discuss what to do here.)

## Mason Street

The adventurers know enough about the town layout to find the best route to Tarry Street and the mentioned headquarters. The route involves going down Mason Street, turning right onto Danbury Road, and finally turning left onto Tarry Street.

As you progress along the street, you get a feeling that someone is following you. Upon looking around, it's just a bunch of everyday folk, stationed at their stores, or walking around with their families. You don't notice anything out of the ordinary, although something still feels off.

If you want to ask anyone you see about either Mayor Bravo or the Sierras, you can attempt a `Gather Information` skill check. For any roll from 5-19, people will tell you that they are the opposing political factions in town. No new information here. If you roll a 20, someone will suggest that the previous election 5 years ago was wrigged, and that the mayor has deep pockets to make things work in his favor. If you roll below a 5, you will be mocked for not knowing anything about politics in this town.

## Danbury Road

As you continue on your path, you still feel like someone is following you, but still don't see anyone.

Along this road, you notice some posters on the walls about the upcoming election.

"A Vote For Bravo Is A Vote For Success!" The poster goes on to detail the accomplishments of the mayor over the past five years. Some of the achievements sound a bit far-fetched, but you chalk it up to political grandstanding.

"Only Sierra Can Fix Our Town!" This poster shows a middle-aged man wearing a bright blue blazer and a bowler hat. He holds out his hand in which he is carrying a hammer. The description suggests that things have gotten worse for the town as of late, and that this former carpenter knows how to fix things.

You consider how these statements seem contradictory, but accept that as how politics works.

These posters are plastered on many surfaces all down the road. You think about how annoying that must be to anyone commuting here daily.

## Tarry Street

As you turn onto Tarry Street, you immediately notice a bright banner on a nearby building that reads, "Sierra Party Election Headquarters". There are posters promoting the party all over the place. It's hard to even find the front door because of this.

Upon approaching the building, a guard stops you.

"Excuse me, but do you have an appointment? You'd better not be here to cause any trouble."

The adventurers can admit that they have no appointment, or they can lie. Either way, the guard will ask another guard to go report / check to confirm the appointment. This will, of course, not go well for the adventurers.

"I'll have to ask you to leave. If you want information regarding the Sierra Party, take a look at the posters."

If the adventurers refuse to leave (ex. "But we have an investigation to run!" or "We need to search the area for a potential criminal"), there will be a fight. The Sierra Guard is listed in the [enemies](./enemies.md) file.

Otherwise, the adventurers will have to figure out another way to complete this mission. Perhaps sneaking around to the back door? Another guard, who attacks immediately to force a fight.

Once defeated, the guard advises the group that there are more guards inside, and that anyone looking for trouble will instead find only defeat.

## Sierra Party Headquarters

The group enters, either through the front or the back door, and finds themselves in a large room filled with equipment. It looks like they are planning a victory parade with these strangely colorful machines on wheels. There are also stacks of flyers like the ones posted outside. Nothing seems to be particularly suspicious in here.

You hear some noise from upstairs, and head up there to see if you can find out more about the accusations. There is a room with double-doors, and two guards in front of it.

The guards notice you immediately, even if you try to sneak up there.

"Oh, the new volunteers? Great! You should go in and meet Romeo Sierra. He'll give you all the details on your job."

Given the stroke of luck, the adventurers should know not to argue or correct the guard. You head on in to the room.

## Romeo Sierra

"Ah, welcome! So glad you could make it on short notice. I'm Romeo Sierra, leader of the Sierra Party. I'm sure you're well versed in both my personal history as well as our party mantra, so no need for a more formal introduction. And since you answered my job posting for just such a group of adventurers, I'm sure you don't need too much instruction either."

"To recap, I'm convinced that someone is plotting something nefarious against me. I received a letter in the mail today 'requesting' my presence ALONE in the town center at 9pm tonight. This does not read like a joke. Someone is trying to capture me, because our election campaign is going well and they don't want us to win. How better to prevent our victory than to have the candidate be a no-show at the inauguration? Surely you care about our cause, and know exactly who is behind this devious prank. I don't even need to mention his name. He must be taken care of at once!"

"I see you are looking quite confused. Don't worry, I will provide you the address of the mayor's office. Dig around in there and see what you can find out for me. Report back as soon as you can. Remember - we have a deadline!"

"Now scurry off, I have a victory speech to write!" Romeo escorts you out of the office.

The adventurers go back down the stairs. They decide it's not wise to discuss the matter at hand within earshot of the guards upstairs, so they step outside through whichever door they came in through. At which point, one of the adventurers is grabbed from behind, hand over mouth to suppress any verbal warning, and dragged into a back alley.

## The Mysterious Follower

"What did he tell you? I need answers!" The woman holding you threatens you with a sap (a non-lethal weapon used to knock someone out).

If the adventurer tries to stall, they will get knocked unconscious. They should instead try to explain that both candidates feel threatened by letters they received, advising a meeting in the town center tonight, and now they aren't sure who, if anyone, to believe.

"Well of course! _I_ sent those letters!"

At this point, whether the explanation has been given or the adventurer is unconscious, the rest of the party finds the mysterious follower and confronts them.

"Good job so far, everyone. Let's not cause any disturbances in this lovely alley, shall we? I suppose an introduction is in order. My name is Lima Papa, a journalist for the Towne Tribune. Why is it spelled with an 'e'? I don't know. But I do know that the citizens of this town deserve better! And I'm going to get it for them!"

The adventurers can ask about the journalist's goals or about the letter inviting the two candidates to the town center.

"Isn't it obvious? I need to do interviews for my election article! These candidates can say whatever they want in their propaganda, but they need to be able to answer the questions that the citizens have! I've already done my homework in collecting questions for them from the people that their policies affect. Now I just have to get their answers, in an exclusive interview! And if I can get both of them to answer the same questions, it's so much easier to compare and contrast. This way, the voters have enough information to make an _informed_ decision."

"So that's why I've been following you. I need your help. Get them to appear tonight at 9pm. Then I'll have just enough time to write up my prize-winning story for publication tomorrow!"

The adventurers can discuss how to do this. Since they're already outside the Sierra Headquarters, it makes sense to start there.

## Romeo's Reaction

The adventurers go back upstairs to the double-doored room. The guards stop them.

"Forget something?"

The adventurers should explain that they've already found the information that Romeo was looking for. The guards are increduluous, and ready their weapons.

Romeo flings open the doors and greets the adventurers.

"You're back ALREADY? I had no idea how motivated you volunteers could be. This is incredible!!! Come on in, come on in!"

The adventurers here have an opportunity to explain what they discovered - who is behind the letter, and what the purpose of it is.

"Oh, what an incredible opportunity to better broadcast our message to the public! I love it! I'll need to prepare another speech! Guards! Be ready to accompany me to the town square at 9pm! I'm sure no one will try anything, but I'll feel better with your presence. Oh yes, thank you, volunteers. If you would like to be there too to support me tonight, I'd be happy to have the audience!"

The adventurers agree to be there, and exit the building to plot their next move.

## Busting Buster

The adventurers need to get back to the mayor's office to report what they discovered. They know the way, so if they decide to, they can go back there.

Upon arrival, the door guards recognize them from earlier in the day, and let them enter.

"Excellent, I trust you have the information I requested?"

The adventurers should report their findings, explaining Lima Papa's letter and the interview tonight.

"Lima Papa? The renowned journalist for the Towne Tribune? Why didn't she just explain that in her letter or even sign it? It sounds like a trap. How can you be sure this person you met was telling the truth?"

Good luck. `:)`

"I don't believe you! I think you weren't able to find anything out and you made up this lie! Or worse, you're with the Sierras! GUARDS!"

Two guards enter the room and a fight begins. The [Bravo Guards](./enemies.md) can be found in the enemies list.

"All right, all right, I'll do it. But I'm bringing these guards. Even if what you say is true, I can't trust that Sierra isn't up to something. If you really are telling the truth, you will be there tonight at 9pm. Now get out!"

## The Interview

The adventurers head over to the town center as the evening closes in. It's getting dark. They check around the area to ensure there is no ambush waiting for the candidates. As you're scoping out the area, you are, of course, ambushed by none other than Lima Papa!

"Good evening adventurers, and welcome to the D E B A T E! I trust you got both of the candidates to agree?"

Lima seems in good spirits and is confident the event will go well.

"Excellent! I just need your help preparing the stage."

She asks you to help with moving some things and setting up chairs and desks. She has her own notebook and pen, ready for jotting down the key points for her article.

As 9pm approaches, both Buster Bravo and Romeo Sierra show up, accompanied by a pair of guards. They both nod to you as they notice the group of adventurers, and the guards stick closely to them as they eye the opponent's guards.

"Oh I'm so excited! This is gonna be great! Mayor Bravo, you take this chair, and Mr. Sierra, you can take this chair. I'll sit here at my desk and ask you questions. Answer honestly but succinctly. You'll both get the same questions, in alternating fashion, and feel free to respond to what the other has proposed AFTER THEY ARE FINISHED WITH THEIR ANSWER. Got it? Let's get started!"

The adventurers watch the debate / interview, and find themselves engrossed in the deluge of information and opinion. They come out better informed about the town and its issues, and are proud to have played a role in enabling Lima to write an article that they hope will inspire both sides of the political spectrum.
