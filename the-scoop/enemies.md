# Enemies

Based on the story, the adventurers might encounter some enemies and fight them. This file describes the enemies and the attacks that they use. Remember that enemies attack once per round unless otherwise stated, and their speed determines the order with regards to the adventurers. In case of ties, the adventurers go first.

## Sierra Guard

### Description

The guard is tall and wields a large war hammer (to match the symbol of the Sierra Party). He does not particularly seem to want to fight, but feels a strong duty to protect the Sierra Party Headquarters.

### Combat

The guard has 30 `HP` and a speed of 1. If the adventurers tried to sneak in the back, he attacks first and uses his "Block the Entrance" attack on the first move. He can use the following attacks:

- To Our Victory! - deal 1d6 damage to all adventuers
- Block the Entrance - deal 1d10 damage to one adventurer and make them skip their next turn
- Parry - the next attack against him deals no damage and deals 1d4 damage back to the attacker

## Bravo Guards

### Description

There are two guards who look like fighting veterans. They may have been recruited from gangs or from the prisons, as they look experienced but don't recall any news of the mayor needed to be defended. One has a shield, and the other one has a sword. Neither one seems to have any hesitation about fighting, and so they go first.

### Combat

Each guard has 30 `HP` and a `Speed` of 1. They attack first on the first turn. They choose targets at random and can use the following moves:

- Mercenary Strike - deal 1d10 damage to one adventurer
- Shield Defense - prevent the next 5 damage dealt to either guard
- Corrupt Charge - deal 1d4 damage to all adventurers
