# Enemies

Based on the story, the adventurers might encounter some enemies and fight them. This file describes the enemies and the attacks that they use. Remember that enemies attack once per round unless otherwise stated, and their speed determines the order with regards to the adventurers. In case of ties, the adventurers go first.

## Farmer's Drunk Son

### Description

This adult man is clearly not helping his family with the farm work, choosing the bottle over work. He stands about six feet tall, can't walk straight, or speak without sluring his words. He looks pretty strong, but there is the influence of alcohol to balance that out.

### Combat

He has 10 `HP` for each adventurer in the party, and -3 `Speed`. He has the following attacks:

- Punch - deal 1d6 damage to one adventurer
- Drunken Fury - 50% chance to deal 1d12 damage to an adventurer, 50% chance to deal it to himself
- Scream - Prevents one adventurer from taking any actions next turn (by yelling at them)

This encounter should be easy. If the drunk would knock out any adventurer, they can be set to 1 `HP` instead.

## Gang

### Description

There is one gang member for each adventurer. They are all men, in their late teens and early twenties. They look a little bit worn out, perhaps from an earlier encounter.

This fight will be more difficult. Each one has 15 `HP`. The adventurers should take them out one by one, so there are less opportunities for them to attack.

### Combat

Each gang member has +1 `Speed`, and will choose an adventurer to attack at random.

- Don't Mess With Me - knife attack to deal 1d6 damage to an adventurer
- You Think You're Smart, Punk? - The next gang member's next attack deals triple damage but has a 50% chance to miss
- Taunt - Direct all attacks for the rest of the round to this character

## Monster Creator

### Description

You are not sure if this is a man or a woman. As described in the story, they are tall, slender, and have shoulder-length blonde hair. They wear a black robe and black pants, with a dark red top, and a matching hat. They are clearly a magic user, as the air cackles with electricity around them.

### Combat

`HP` 150
`Speed` 2

Attacks:

- Revenge - Attacks the last adventurer to deal the Monster Creator damage; deals 2d8 damage
- Channel Rage - Attacks all adventurers for 1d6 damage
- Stupefy - All adventurers have a 50% chance to miss their next attack
