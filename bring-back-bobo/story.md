# Bring Back BoBo

## Opening Scene

You and your companions are leaving the inn after a fine breakfast. You notice a woman looking around frantically near the entrance of the inn. She appears to look toward the ground mostly, and sometimes crouches down to get a better look.

(You can choose to approach her. If you do not, she will approach you.)

"BoBo, where are you? Oh, excuse me, kind adventurers. Have you seen my precious BoBo? She's a gray cat, about 15 pounds, rather chubby. Did you see her in the inn?"

(You did not see the cat in the inn.)

"I have no idea where she could have gone! I didn't even open the door this morning until after I noticed she was missing. Not in any of her hiding places. Not in any of her favorite sleeping spots. But there's no way she could have gotten out. I can only suspect that something terrible has happened. Please! I need you to find her. I will give you a monetary reward for doing so, assuming you bring her back in one piece. Can you find my BoBo?"

(You should accept. Otherwise, why are you playing?)

The woman gives you her address, and asks if you want to search her house.

(Players should discuss what to do here.)

## The Owner's House

If the group chooses to investigate the woman's house, they will have plenty of places to search, but ultimately come up empty. Notably, the house is covered in cat toys, cat pictures, cat furniture, and of course, cat hair. The woman will apologize for the mess, noting how impossible it is to clean that stuff up.

There is a kitchen, a dining room, a living room, two bedrooms upstairs, a bathroom upstairs, and a basement. BoBo's favorite hiding spots are in the bathroom upstairs and the basement. She is frequently (not hiding) on the sofa in the living room, or shoving her face full of food in the kitchen. The woman casually notes how much BoBo eats, and how much she enjoys it. BoBo is, of course, neither hiding in any of these spots, nor in the public spaces. If you choose to search the basement extremely carefully, you will find all sorts of cat toys hidden in various nooks and crannies down there. But no cat.

The only other major thing to note is that the woman's story checks out - the doors are all shut tight, and the windows are not open. There are no holes in the walls or any other way for the cat to get out.

## The Villagers

With seemingly nothing to go on, you resort to asking around the neighborhood, if anyone has seen BoBo. (If the adventurers do not come up with this idea on their own, you might suggest it to them.)

There are some people out and about in the neighborhood, some in front of their houses, and others along the street. The players can talk to any or all of the outdoor villagers. This would require a `Gather Information` skill check. All neighbors will talk on any roll. A critical failure (a 1 on the d20, regardless of modifier) will have them make fun of you, or make fun of your description of BoBo (for being sooooo big). For example, "You must be desperate if you're asking me. That cat sounds massive! How could you lose her?" Be creative! Any other roll will give you the first set of dialogue. Good follow-up questions (DM can judge) or a 16+ initial roll will give you both sets of dialogue.

### The Dog Walker

"Have I seen BoBo? Plenty of times! That cat is so lazy! She just lies on the sofa all day, getting up only to eat. I love visiting and petting her. Sometimes she seems a bit upset but won't do anything like bite or scratch. Friendly cat! Oh, you mean today. Nope."

"That woman is really obsessed with her cat. A little too much, if you ask me. But it's all in good spirit, and I do like that she gives me presents just for visiting her. I'm not sure if she's trying to buy friends, or if she's that naive to be so nice to everyone, but I can assure you, she is a good person. She's not pulling your leg. If she says BoBo just disappeared, didn't run away, she's telling the truth."

### The Joggers

"No, I haven't seen a cat today. Sorry. Gotta run! Get it?"

"Don't know that woman too well. Can't say if BoBo is an indoor only cat or if she goes outside regularly."

### The Gardener

"A cat? Haven't seen it. Go away."

"Look, I don't like cats, ok!? Them feral cats digging up my vegetables. Wish they'd just chase off the birds and the squirrels, but no, they want to dig. Big bunch a jerks. Good riddance for them getting lost!"

### The Kid

"Nopers, I didn't see any cats this morning."

"Oh, but you want to know something weird? I saw this really pretty person walking by today. Never seen them before in this town. They were wearing a black robe and a red hat. Thin build with shoulder-length blonde hair. Couldn't tell if they were a boy or a girl. My mommy told me not to talk to strangers, so I didn't ask them to play with me. They went that way (pointing down the street leading out of town)."

## Following the Road Out of Town

Given that the kid's observation is the only thing to go on, the adventurers should follow that lead. If they choose instead to do something else like ask around the inn or something, no one knows anything about BoBo. No one else has seen this strange person.

As you continue down the road, you come upon a farm. There are some workers preparing the land in the distance. But you also notice a strange person stumbling around near the road. Your walking is clearly "disturbing" him, and he approaches you.

"This is MY road! I am the king of the road! Don't need no home, got it right here. I go wherever the wind takes me." Upon saying this, he falls to the ground. The wind clearly took him to the ground, even though there is no distinctive breeze. "Ah, such a good bed. It is getting late, hic, after all."

Let the adventurers be creative here.

If they choose to ask about BoBo, the man will ramble drunkenly.

If they inform the man that it's the morning, not getting late...

"You probably sleep all night, that's the problem with you! You don't understand what I mean by late, because you slept through the morning! Your perspective is all wrong. Everyone else is wrong! I am right!"

If they try to help the man, there will be a fight. He is a proud drunk, and doesn't want help from anyone. He will attempt to fight in a chaotic manner. This is meant to be an introduction to combat, if the players choose this path. It will be easy. The Farmer's Drunk Son is listed in the [enemies](./enemies.md) file.

If the adventurers choose to leave him alone, or after the fight, he will remember something and call out to you.

"Hey! I just remembered something! Thought it was a dream, but I must be sobering up. There was this gorgeous woman who I ran into a bit earlier. Literally ran into. She wasn't happy with that. I was awestruck by her beautiful blonde hair. Sleek black robe, check. I made an advance but she was still unhappy about me bumping into her, and somehow I wound up face down in the dirt. Don't know what happened. But yeah, let me know if you see her, yeah? She was headed that way (he points away from the farm, in the direction you were heading)."

## The Bandit Gang

As the adventurers continue down the road past the farm, they see a small group of people up ahead. Since the surrounding area is flat, and there is no cover, there is no way to "avoid" them.

"Hey, lookie here. A group of 'adventurers'. How boring."

"SOOOOOO boring after that gorgeous babe."

The adventurers realize they are dealing with some sort of gang or thugs. If any of the adventurers identifies as female, the group will try to pick a fight.

"Hey you, you're pretty easy on the eyes. You're gonna keep us company."

"Yeah, until we're done with you, HEH HEH HEH!"

The Gang is listed in the [enemies](./enemies.md) file.

After the fight, or if there is no fight due to the party composition, you can ask the gang members about BoBo.

"Nah, we ain't seen no cat. Still thinking about that hot chick though. Shame she wouldn't come play with us, if you know what I mean."

"Yeah, next thing we knew, we were pushed out of the way. Didn't see her push us though. Maybe it was a good thing she didn't want to hang out with us. Who knows what she'd be into?"

If you ask more about that woman they saw, you confirm that she is the same one the drunk and the kid mentioned. She continued down the road, so you follow suit.

## The Crowd

As you continue on the road, you see another village up ahead, although it's still a bit in the distance. What's more important is that there is a group of people visible near the edge of the town. They are pointing up, not quite at the sky, but as if there were something above the buildings.

As you approach, you can make out some of what they are saying.

"It crushes everything beneath it! We're doomed!"

"It ate all my sausages! Now what am I going to sell for a living?"

"Where could something like that have come from? What did we do to deserve this?"

"Perhaps this is judgement day."

You can ask them about what's going on.

"A monster appeared in our town today. We all had to flee for our lives! It doesn't seem interested in us, but it is wreaking havoc on our village. Please help us!"

"EEEEEKKKKK!!! THERE IT IS!!!!"

You look in the direction the villager is pointing, and you see a huge monster!

## The Monster

At present, the monster seems to be looking around the town. It towers above the buildings, standing roughly 30 feet tall. Most buildings in the village are one or two stories (8 or 16 feet), for comparison purposes.

As the monster emerges into the main path through town, you realize the monster looks a lot like a 30 foot tall house cat. It's gray and rotund. You realize this is BoBo, just on a different scale than what the owner described.

BoBo lets out a roaring "reow", which knocks the party and the onlookers to the ground. She turns and marches toward the center of the town. Clearly, she needs to be stopped, and the villagers aren't going to cut it.

The adventurers should discuss their plan of action here. Yes, BoBo can be attacked, but the owner would be devastated if she wasn't brought back as a house cat. What happened here? Can we incapacitate her? Can we return her to house cat size?

While the adventurers might need some help here, let them be creative and form a plan. Don't worry, there's no way your attacks are going to really hurt her. There's kind of a size difference here. You do need to stop her from stomping on the town, so try something! There's no actual "combat" here. BoBo is unaffected by all damaging attacks, and she either cries, knocking everyone to the ground again, or swipes at them to get them out of the way. However, it does stall her advance, and someone takes notice.

## The Culprit

"What are you doing to my creation?"

The blonde haired person in a black robe and red hat steps out from an alleyway. The voice sounds masculine, but a bit higher pitch than what you would expect from a man. You are still not sure if they are a man or a woman.

The adventurers should confront this person.

"BoBo? Oh, you mean the cat I teleported here? I needed a vessel for my rage, and I found one. Yes, a cat who demolishes anything in her path, with one goal. While her goal may not have been revenge, mine is! She was willing, and my scan of the area found her desires compatible with mine. Thus BoBo became the monster you see before you. Cower, and flee before my rage incarnate!"

If the adventurers ask what happened or for more information, this person will not want to talk. Either way, a fight will start, but this time, you face the [Monster Creator](./enemies.md).

## The Aftermath

"How surprising. You managed to defeat me. Perhaps, then, I can ask for your help? I'll explain what led up to this, and I'll return BoBo, if you'll hear me out."

The adventurers should listen. It will be fun.

"Thank you. I hope you will understand. We've all been bullied and made fun of in our lives, right? Even as an adult, it keeps happening to me. But now it's not about getting beaten up, it's about being hit on. Not hit as in violence, but as in unwelcome advances."

"For some reason, people think I'm a girl. And wherever I go, I get these ugly men trying to touch me or at least making snide remarks about my appearance. I can't help the way I look. This is who I am. And I don't go around trying to get men to notice me. I just want to live my life. And yet I can't do something as simple as go between two towns without gathering attention from the farm drunk and a group of thugs. I can't go to the inn here in town without a group of men surrounding me, even though they should know that I'm a guy, since I live here."

"I figured the only way anyone would take me seriously was to prove that I could fight back. And that's when I came up with the plan to attack the town with a monster. I needed someone who was HUNGRY for power, and I found this cat while casting a scanning spell. She happily accepted my offer. I brought her here through teleportation, cast a growth spell on her, and set her loose in the town. THAT WILL TEACH THEM NOT TO MESS WITH ME!!!!"

The man starts laughing, with tears rolling down his beautiful face. You can tell he is in emotional pain. The adventurers should try to help him, asking what they can do, or suggesting other ways he can prevent this from happening in the future. For example, "What if you just wore a shirt saying, 'I'm a MAN'", or "What if you just introduced yourself to these people and provided your gender/pronouns?", or "What if you grew some facial hair?". Have fun, be silly, and find a way to get him to realize he doesn't need to crush his home town, although he is quite welcome to crush the thugs and the drunk.

As this comes to a silly conclusion, you realize that the spell on BoBo has been broken. She is now coming up to the group, begging for food. You should take her home.

It is late in the afternoon as you return to the original town, with BoBo following along, normal sized. You go to the owner's house, and she lets out a squeal as she realizes that you have brought BoBo home!

"Don't worry, my little one, your dinner is all ready for you in your favorite food bowl in the kitchen."

You realize she has more than one food bowl, and worry that this person is overfeeding her. No wonder she was found by a spell seeking hunger.

"Where was she? Tell me about how you found her!"

You relay the story to her, and she seems particularly intrigued.

"Tell that man to come visit me. I will be happy to have him as a guest, and I already know that he'll get along with BoBo. Anyway, thank you so much for returning her. Here is the reward, as promised. And be sure to pass that message along! I'd love to meet him. He sounds charming."

You aren't sure what to make of all of this, see yourselves out, and head back to the inn for dinner.

--THE END--
