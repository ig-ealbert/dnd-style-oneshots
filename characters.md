# List of Premade Characters

These are designed for first-time or beginner players who don't want to build their own characters. Everything is simplified to be easier to get started quickly. Players can decide on character names, genders, appearances, backstories, etc. These characters start at level 1 and can be modified for subsequent adventures to have "leveled up" (stats increased).

## Warrior - Miran Heavyaxe

### Description

You are a dwarven fighter. Your family was attacked by a group of bandits, and while you were able to defend your family, it made you decide to take the fight to the badguys, wherever they may be. You cannot allow evildoers to terrorize any innocents, whether your family or others. You specialize in weapon-based combat, using your family's axe that has been passed down through generations - Skull Splitter.

### Alignment

Chaotic Good

### Stats

- `HP` 30
- `Strength` +3
- `Speed` -1
- `Smarts` 0
- `Charisma` -1
- `Endurance` +4

### Equipment

- "Skull Splitter" (battle axe) - gives +2 to any abilities using it
- Leather Armor - reduces damage taken by 1
- Shield of the Dwarves - reduces damage taken by 2 when an ability uses it

### Abilities - Class Specific

- SKULL SMASH! - Attack one enemy with your axe for 1d8 + `Strength` damage
- Axe Whirlwind - Spin around and attack all enemies with your axe for 1d4 + `Strength` damage
- Shields up! - Jump in front of an attack block with your shield (damage reduced also by `Endurance`)

### Abilities - Generic

- Gather Information - Ask around for details, hints, clues, or stories. 1d20 + `Charisma`.
- Sense Motive - Figure out if someone is lying. 1d20 + `Smarts`.

## Cleric - Blinda Soother

### Description

You are an elven cleric, devoted to healing those in need. Your desire to help others has led you to travel to better service those who are hurt. You specialize in holy magic, and can unleash it upon your enemies when necessary. Your staff channels both healing and holy lightning.

### Alignment

Lawful Good

### Stats

- `HP` 15
- `Strength` -1
- `Speed` +1
- `Smarts` +2
- `Charisma` +2
- `Endurance` -1

### Equipment

- Healing Staff - used for either healing allies or channeling lightning to damage foes
- Leather Armor - reduces damage taken by 1
- Potion of Healing (S) x3 - consumable (it goes away after use) to heal any character 10 HP

### Abilities - Class Specific

- Holy Bolt - Attack one enemy with your staff's lightning for 1d6 + `Smarts` damage
- Patch You Up - Heal one enemy with your staff for 1d8 `HP`
- Don't Be Mean - Create a light shield to prevent up to the next 1d10 damage dealt to a friendly character

### Abilities - Generic

- Gather Information - Ask around for details, hints, clues, or stories. 1d20 + `Charisma`.
- Sense Motive - Figure out if someone is lying. 1d20 + `Smarts`.

## Rogue - Macky McSneak

### Description

You are a human rogue, a sneaky trickster with a penchant for knives. You prefer to avoid direct confrontations, but do wield your weapons when necessary. Avoiding detection, noticing things others don't, and stealing anything you might find particularly shiny are your joys in life. You haven't been caught so far, and don't intend to be in the future.

### Alignment

Chaotic Neutral

### Stats

- `HP` 18
- `Strength` 1
- `Speed` +2
- `Smarts` +2
- `Charisma` 0
- `Endurance` -2

### Equipment

- Dagger - Used for attack when necessary, but much better as a threat to someone's throat
- Sneaky Cloak - Increases evasion - 1d20 + `Speed` check whenever attacked; dodges on 18+
- Potion of Poison (S) x2 - consumable (it goes away after use) to deal 3 damage each of the next 5 turns

### Abilities - Class Specific

- Stabby Stab - Attack one enemy with your dagger for 1d4 + `Speed` damage
- Oh Really? - Sneak up and threaten an enemy with your dagger; 1d20 + `Speed` check; enemy skips next attack on 16+
- Trap Check - Checks for traps in the area - 1d20 + `Smarts`; succeeds on 10+

### Abilities - Generic

- Gather Information - Ask around for details, hints, clues, or stories. 1d20 + `Charisma`.
- Sense Motive - Figure out if someone is lying. 1d20 + `Smarts`.

## Ranger - Veric Bowspeed

### Description

You are an elven ranger. Your bow takes down enemies from a distance. The wilds are your preferred environment, so you feel out of touch with nature in a city. You wish to protect nature and the animals that have befriended you. When animals are close by, you can command them to attack. You prefer to scout and observe from the high ground, keeping stealth and distance as allies.

### Alignment

Neutral Good

### Stats

- `HP` 24
- `Strength` +2
- `Speed` +2
- `Smarts` +1
- `Charisma` 0
- `Endurance` -2

### Equipment

- Wooden Bow - Ranged attack
- Leather Armor - Reduces damage taken by 1
- Scout's Binoculars - Gives +3 to Scout the Area ability

### Abilities - Class Specific

- Long Shot - Attack one enemy with your bow for 1d6 + `Strength` damage
- Warning Shot - Intentionally miss an enemy, scaring them into dealing 1d6 less damage next attack
- Scout the Area - Learn more about the surrounding area; 1d20; succeeds on 10+; extra info on 18+

### Abilities - Generic

- Gather Information - Ask around for details, hints, clues, or stories. 1d20 + `Charisma`.
- Sense Motive - Figure out if someone is lying. 1d20 + `Smarts`.

## Bard - Wika Songift

### Description

You are a gnomish musician and storyteller. You are extremely knowledgeable about cultures and locations, as you have learned about them through song and story. You're great with people and a smooth talker. You inspire your allies with songs of strength, and discourage foes with songs of fear. Fighting is not your style, but you lend a helping hand wherever you can, often in exchange for coin.

### Alignment

Neutral Good

### Stats

- `HP` 22
- `Strength` 0
- `Speed` +1
- `Smarts` +2
- `Charisma` +3
- `Endurance` -3

### Equipment

- Songstrung Lute - Musical instrument for battle spells
- Leather Armor - Reduces damage taken by 1

### Abilities - Class Specific

- Song of Strength - Play a song to power up your allies; they deal 1d6 extra damage this combat (one use per combat)
- Song of Fear - Play a song to scare your enemies; they deal 1d4 less damage this combat (one use per combat)
- Lesser Heal - Use music to heal an ally for 1d10 `HP`.

### Abilities - Generic

- Gather Information - Ask around for details, hints, clues, or stories. 1d20 + `Charisma`.
- Sense Motive - Figure out if someone is lying. 1d20 + `Smarts`.

## Mage - Sierra Burncast

You are prideful and enjoy showing off your abilities, much to the dismay of your enemies. You'll make sure that everyone knows you can burn twice as bright without any drawbacks.

### Alignment

Chaotic Neutral

### Stats

- `HP` 18
- `Strength` -2
- `Speed` +1
- `Smarts` +4
- `Charisma` +2
- `Endurance` -3

### Equipment

- Fire Staff - Gives fire attacks +2 damage
- Mage Cloak - Reduces damage taken by 1
- Potion of Fire (S) x2 - consumable (it goes away after use) to deal 1d10 damage to an enemy

### Abilities - Class Specific

- Fireball - Scorch one enemy for 1d8 damage
- Heatwave - Engulf all enemies in flames for 1d4 damage
- Illusion - Create an illusory copy of an ally; 1d20 + `Charisma` - next enemy attacks it on 15+

### Abilities - Generic

- Gather Information - Ask around for details, hints, clues, or stories. 1d20 + `Charisma`.
- Sense Motive - Figure out if someone is lying. 1d20 + `Smarts`.
