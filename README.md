# Dungeons and Dragons Style Oneshot Adventures

## Who, What, and Why

I wanted to create a D&D-style story for a friend who was thinking of getting into D&D with his children, but had no experience with it. Something that would be fun and simple enough for newcomers. Something with reduced complexity. Also, I don't have reference materials for creating actual D&D characters or gameplay, so this will be in a similar style but simplified.

## Where

There is a list of premade characters at the root level, as they can be used for any beginner adventure. Each adventure will be in its own folder. It will have a list of enemies for the DM (Dungeon Master - the story teller). It will have a story which the DM should read ahead of time. It will need to be modified based on the player choices and whether or not they are first-timers using the level 1 characters.

## When and How

These are designed for first-time players, and can be played any time! You'll likely need between 1-2 hours to get through the story after explaining the basic rules of the game, which might take about 10 minutes.

Make sure you have dice! The characters and enemies will list the dice they use.

- d4 = 4 sided die
- d6 = 6 sided die
- d8 = 8 sided die
- d10 = 10 sided die
- d12 = 12 sided die
- d20 = 20 sided die
