# Enemies

Based on the story, the adventurers might encounter some enemies and fight them. This file describes the enemies and the attacks that they use. Remember that enemies attack once per round unless otherwise stated, and their speed determines the order with regards to the adventurers. In case of ties, the adventurers go first.

## Woodland Sprites

### Description

These are playful, devious little sprites. They fly. Their size is about 1 foot tall. They have blue skin and sheer wings. There are sparkles emanating from their wings, indicating magic.

### Combat

There is one sprite per adventurer (ex. 4 sprites for a party of 4 adventurers), each with 10 `HP` and +2 `Speed`. They choose targets at random, and can use the following attacks:

- Slap and Giggle - deal 1d6 damage to one adventurer
- Pixie Dust - deal 1d4 damage to one adventurer and reduce their next attack damage by the same amount
- You Can't Catch Me! - the next weapon attack misses; magic attacks and effects still hit

It would be wise for the adventurers to take out the sprites one by one, but they have to figure that out.

## Novice Thief

### Description

There is a young human man about five and a half feet tall, with dark brown hair and brown eyes. He is dressed in a brown traveler's robe, and looks very inconspicuous. He carries a knapsack, looking like a traveler. He has drawn a knife, looking to cut loose any valuable items you may be carrying. Guess that knapsack is filled with stolen goods.

### Combat

The young man works with the elderly woman as a distraction, but the combat is only with the man. He has 60 `HP` and +1 `Speed`. Because the adventurers were surprised by him, he goes first in combat. He chooses targets at random, and can use the following attacks:

- Helping With Your Heavy Pockets - knife attack to deal 1d10 damage to an adventurer
- Fan of Knives - deal 1d4 + `Speed` damage to all adventurers
- Misdirection - dodge the next two attacks

## Festival Guards

### Description

There are 2 guards summoned to catch the "thieves" (the adventurers) if they cannot reimburse the merchant for the meat that Zulu stole. They are meant to keep the peace at the festival, but are willing to fight anyone who disturbs the enjoyment of their town's special day.

### Combat

Each guard has these stats, and attacks an adventurer at random.

`HP` 75
`Speed` 2

Attacks:

- Protect the City - Attacks one adventurer for 2d8 damage and negates the next attack against this guard
- Crowd Control - Attacks all adventurers for 1d6 damage
- Restraining Order - One adventurer cannot deal damage to the guards next turn
