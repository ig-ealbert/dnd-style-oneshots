# Search for Zulu

## Opening Scene

You and your companions are at the inn, eating delicious food and drinking the house special, sharing stories and laughing together, when a frazzled man bursts into the inn. He quickly scans the tables and finds your group. He approaches with haste.

"Oh good! You seem like a group of competent adventurers. I need your help. My dog, Zulu, has gone missing. He was in my room when I woke up, but after I finished getting ready for the day and was ready to give him his breakfast, he was nowhere to be found! He must have gotten out somehow. He's always been such a well behaved dog, and a very loving family pet. I can't imagine why he would just up and leave! I wonder if someone took him away? Please! I need you to find him. I will give you a monetary reward for doing so, assuming you bring him back in one piece. Can you find him for me?"

The man looks desperate, and he is offering a decent reward for the simple act of finding a lost dog, so you accept.

"Oh thank goodness! His name is Zulu, and he is a large black and brown dog with short hair. He weighs roughly 80 pounds, and loves meat. Here is a picture of him. Please, please find him quickly. My house is right off a nearby street, so it's likely that the merchants along this road might have seen him and where he went. Here's my address. Please bring him back to my home safely. I'm counting on you!"

With that, the adventuring group finishes up their meal, and discusses the job they just accepted.

(Players should discuss what to do here.)

## The Roadside Merchants

There are a few merchants near the inn along the main road in town. The players can talk to any or all of the merchants. This would require a `Gather Information` skill check. All merchants will talk on any roll. A critical failure (a 1 on the d20, regardless of modifier) will have them mock you about needing to ask about such a simple task. For example, "You must be desperate if you're asking me. How hard is it to find a dog?" Be creative! Any other roll will give you the first set of dialogue. Good follow-up questions (DM can judge) or a 16+ initial roll will give you both sets of dialogue.

### The Blacksmith

"This morning I was slammed with orders from demanding clients. I was busy in the back of my shop, melting and molding. I didn't see no dog, and it was loud enough that I wouldn't have heard any barking. Now leave me to my work!"

### The Produce Vendor

"Oh yes, that's right. Early this morning, right after I opened shop, I had a few customers looking to cherry pick the best produce that had just arrived. This is typical, as I set out the newly arrived produce before I open up. One of them dropped an apple they were inspecting. It fell to the ground and got bruised to the point where I couldn't sell it, even at a discount. When I asked for them to pay for the damaged goods, they gave me an excuse, or so I thought. Said a dog ran right by them at a breakneck pace, startling them and causing them to drop the apple, as if it wasn't their fault. Ugh, another loss for my business, but at least it was only one apple."

"No, I don't know which way the dog went. The customer might have known, but I have no recollection of what they looked like."

### The Trinket Salesman

"A dog? Well, the only thing out of the ordinary that I noticed this morning was a flash of gold! As you can see, I have an exceptional eye for valuables. While I was bargaining with a customer this morning, I saw a blur and a flash of gold behind them. No idea what it was, but it happened. I don't miss gold."

"It came from my left to my right, which would mean whatever it was headed west. Good luck!"

### The Cook

"That dog! Yes, I saw him. But I don't like him. I pride myself on creating the best stews and lunches this town has ever known, and you know what that dog did? Ran right by me. Didn't even stop to smell the fabulous aromas. As if he was putting down my hard work. Not even worthy of a dog's smell? Good riddance that he's lost."

## The Owner's House

If the group chooses to investigate the man who came with the request (just to be sure it's not a trap), they will find him, his wife, and his kids waiting at their house. The players can choose to talk to any of them.

The man will simply repeat what he said at the inn, describing his dog as loyal and friendly. He will ask you to take a good look at the picture and hurry to find him.

In the picture, you will notice that Zulu has a distinctive pink collar and a shiny golden name tag.

The wife will paint a slightly different picture. "Zulu is always so attached to my husband. He will play with the children sometimes, but completely ignores me. Who do you think buys and prepares his food? I mean that for both Zulu and my husband. Still, the children love him, and they would be heartbroken were anything bad to happen to him. For their sake, I would ask that you bring him home."

The children will have fun stories. There is a young boy about the age of 5, and a young girl about the age of 8.

The young boy will tell you a story. "I love Zulu! He plays with me! One time, I didn't like what Mom cooked, and so I fed it all to Zulu, and he loved it!!! Don't tell Mom that. She thinks I love her beef and broccoli, but I don't like eating trees, and the beef is too chewy. It's yucky."

The young girl will tell you a story. "Zulu mostly hangs out with Dad, but one time, he came outside to play with me and my friends. We were playing catch with a ball, and he would chase the ball wherever it went! We had to make sure it didn't touch the ground, because if it did, he'd grab it from us! Of course, he eventually jumped up and caught it, and wouldn't give it back. On the bright side, he laid down and let us all pet him as long as he could keep the ball in his mouth. What a silly dog!"

Investigating the home will find you one clue - there is a doggy door to a fenced in backyard. A player might be able to conclude that, based on the description of the dog, he could have jumped the fence if significantly motivated.

## Continuing Down the Main Road

After the merchant district, the town fades into the distance and there is farmland and woods. The woods are deep and dark - there would be no chance of finding Zulu if he went in there. The farmland is vast, and as it is spring, the planting is just starting, so the visibility is open. He's nowhere to be seen on the farms.

As you explore the farmlands, you hear a noise behind you. A group of woodland sprites has emerged from the forest and you they are giggling.

"What are you silly people doing searching the farms? It's spring! There's no food to steal yet! And don't worry, come harvest time, we'll take care of the stealing. Hehe!"

Let the adventurers be creative here.

If they choose to ask about the dog, the sprites seem slightly annoyed, but respond that they did not see a dog.

If they ask follow-up questions or attack the sprites, there will be a fight. The Woodland Sprites are listed in the [enemies](./enemies.md) file. They're playful, and would rather be mischevious than serious, which is why they are not happy with the serious questions.

If the adventurers choose to ignore them and leave, no fight happens.

## The Shepherd

As the adventurers continue down the road to the west, they encounter a group of sheep being led by a shepherd. If they choose to ask about Zulu, the shepherd will provide useful information.

"I did see a dog this morning. I only noticed it because my sheep got nervous and started moving away from the road. When I looked at what might be causing that, I saw a large dog galloping down the road, headed west. Almost seemed like the dog was out of its mind, going all-in on whatever it was after. I'm just glad that it didn't pay any attention to my sheep."

The shepherd doesn't know anything about how much farther the dog went, or what it might have been after. If there are follow up questions beyond that or out of scope, the shepherd asks you to leave him alone to tend to the flock. Before he goes, he will subtly suggest that you heal your party, in case the adventurers didn't think of this before. It's something of a warning for what comes next.

"Be careful on the road, travelers. I'd recommend you heal up, just in case you run into trouble up ahead."

## The Elderly Woman

As you continue on the road, you see a small village up ahead, although it's still a bit in the distance. Before you get to the outskirts, you find an old woman on the side of the road, sitting on a rock. She seems troubled.

If the adventurers choose to talk to her, she will tell a story.

"My aching back! Oh, I knew I shouldn't have gone this far on a walk! I just want to stay healthy, so I decided to go for a stroll. I lost track of time, and found myself here, outside the village. That's when the pain started. I don't think I can make it back home on my own. Would you kind strangers be able to accompany me? I'm not asking for a piggy back ride or anything. Just a couple of strong folks who I can lean on."

If the adventurers choose to do anything further with her (other than say no and move on), everyone should roll a d20 and add their `Speed` modifier (often called a "Perception check"). Anyone who rolls above a 14 will notice that this is a trap. There is a thief behind them, attempting to steal their gold and items. If anyone rolls a 20 on the d20 (without modifiers), they catch the thief immediately and he runs away without a fight. Otherwise, if anyone notices the thief, the entire party will be notified and it will lead to a fight. The [Novice Thief](./enemies.md) can be found in the enemies list.

If no one notices the trap, the adventurers can help the woman walk back to the village. When they drop her off at her house, which she directs them to, she thanks them and gives them a verbal blessing (which has no effect in the game, but might make them feel like they did the right thing). After they leave, they realize that their gold and consumable items have been stolen, which will be a huge detriment to their quest. If you want to be extremely friendly, you might be flexible with the success threshold based on your group's rolls.

When the thief is defeated, he flees. There is no opportunity or advantage to pursuing him. The adventurers can still choose to help the woman back to her home, with no effect on the story either way.

## The Village - Part 1

As you enter the village, you hear a dog bark. It gives you hope! You have no idea if it's Zulu or not, but at least you reminded of the quest, and you feel like you're close.

Let the adventurers explore the village. There are some places in particular that might be helpful.

### The Inn

The innkeeper is surprised to see adventurers in the small village.

"Our village is small and quiet. A great place to raise a family, but not so much to do here for thrillseekers. What brings you folks to town?"

If the adventurers ask about Zulu, the innkeeper warns them.

"Many people have pets here, as it gives them a sense of security and protection. Don't go taking anyone's beloved animal, mistaking it for the one you're after. Might not want to snoop around the residential area either, as people will be suspicious."

### The Food Market

"Are you here to buy some tasty meat? Guaranteed fresh! Actually, I don't have a full selection today. Someone came by yesterday and bought all of my spiced beef. Hadn't seen the young lady around before. Very attractive. It was a bit suspicious, I'll admit, but I don't ask questions when they're not good for my business."

### The Residential Area

There are various residents out and about, and you can ask them without being too instrusive or knocking on doors. Anyone who tries to ask a resident about Zulu while in their house will be "asked" to respect their privacy.

Out around the neighborhood, all adventurers can make one 1d20 roll and ask a resident. Depending on the roll, various answers can be given.

16+:
"Did you hear? The barking? From that house at the corner of Elm and Oak Streets? That guy has never wanted anything to do with dogs before. I wonder if he's finally realized how wonderful they are and taken one in. Maybe we'll get to meet the new dog soon!"

6-15:
"I heard a bark that I didn't recognize today, but I don't know where it came from. It certainly wasn't my Princess. She has the cutest little 'yip'. Isn't that right, Princess? You're just the cutest little doggie!"

2-5:
"I don't know anything about a Zulu. Good luck on your search."

1 (Critical Failure):

"You crazy kids! Get off my lawn!"

"We're not on your lawn, we're on the street. So are you."

"You're close enough, now scram!"

## The Village - Part 2

Hopefully someone got a good roll. You go to investigate the house at the corner of Elm and Oak Streets. As you look around, you notice there is a fenced in backyard, but this fence is chain link and 8ft tall. It's clearly not your normal picket fence for a family yard.

If the adventurers choose to knock on the front door, a voice stops them before they do. They hear it from the back yard, so they go to investigate. If the adventurers choose to wait and observe the back yard, they hear the voice and see who it comes from.

"All right, calm down! I have your food here. Be a good dog for once and calm down! Sheesh! I can't wait until this is over. Then I don't have to prepare any more beef for you, and I don't have to deal with your noise!"

The adventurers see a man in his twenties coming into the fenced in back yard, followed by a large dog with a pink collar and a golden tag. Comparing with the photo, it's definitely Zulu. Zulu himself seems to be hungry, but in good spirits, as the man places a bowl of chopped up meat down on the ground. Zulu eats as if he hasn't been fed all day.

The man starts talking to himself.

"I can't understand why anyone would want a dog if this is what dogs are like. They just demand food, attention, more food, and then you have to clean up after them. Waste of time on all fronts. I hope what that woman said is true, and that she keeps up her end of the bargain. I'm glad that enchantment wore off though, as I never want to deal with a dog under that spell again. Couldn't stay still, tore through that meat like it was a chew toy. He'd better fetch a hefty price."

At this point, the adventurers should decide what to do. If they choose to confront the man now, they will get some dialogue and a fight. If they choose to wait, they will meet their opponent first.

"Now now, I will of course hold up my end of the bargain."

A sorceress emerges from the house alongside the man. She is dressed provacatively, and seems to be using some sort of spell to get him to do what she wants.

"You've done well so far. You traveled with the enchanted meat, attracting a dog to follow you back here. The people in this village love dogs. They will pay an exorbitant amount of money for a strong dog who can protect them, and this one fits the bill exactly. All we have to do is sell the dog at the fair tomorrow, and you'll keep half of the price, as promised. You may even find that you enjoy this type of work, and do it again, but that's up to you."

The man seems hypnotized.

"Yes, my lady. Anything for you."

At this point, hopefully the adventurers will choose to step in. If they chose to confront the man before, the sorceress appears. Either way, the sorceress will taunt them before the fight begins.

"You've made a poor decision challenging me, little adventurers. I can do so much more than simple obsession spells, as you've seen with both this poor man here and the dog who would follow him to the ends of the earth for the enchanted meat. Imagine what I could do to you."

She snaps her fingers, and the adventurers are teleported inside the fenced in yard, for what is effectively a cage match. Ironic considering how dogs are treated. At this point the man seems to regain his senses, and goes to hide inside the house. The [Evil Sorceress](./enemies.md) can be found in the list of enemies.

## The Return

The sorceress is defeated. She snaps her fingers, and disappears. The adventurers are greeted by Zulu, who is happily wagging his tail, smelling his owner on their clothing. The man comes out of the house.

"What... what just happened? Care to explain how you got in my yard? And where did she go?"

The adventurers can explain the situation. Depending on whether or not they got the explanation by observing the back yard longer, the man can explain the plan to sell Zulu and make a profit, as he's desperate for money and the sorceress offered him a way out of debt by selling dogs. Seeing as the man was being taken advantage of and has no desire to deal with dogs, the adventurers should leave him alone and focus on returning Zulu to his owner.

It's the evening now, and the adventurers take Zulu and hasten east back towards the original town. It's close to 22:00 when they arrive at the man's house. They knock on the door, and hear some commotion upstairs as someone scrambles to answer the door.

"ZULU!!!!!!!!!!"

The man darts out of the house and embraces Zulu. Zulu responds joyfully by licking his face and jumping on him.

"I thought you weren't going to come back, given how late it was. Where was he? What happened? Oh, come on in, you must have a great story to tell."

The adventurers go inside the man's house, and regale him with the tale of tracking down Zulu.

"Oh, what an evil scheme, to steal and then sell a dog. I hope this doesn't happen to anyone ever again. It sounds like you scared off that evil sorceress, and hopefully that man will wise up to not be fooled by such promises again."

"Here is the payment, as promised. And as for YOU, Zulu, don't ever run off like that again!"

At this point, the kids come down in their sleep clothes, and happily reunite with their beloved dog.

--THE END--
